Cairn Privacy Policy

Last updated October 16, 2026

Cairn: Words to Keep is made by Jared Apperson. This policy is short because there's little to say: Cairn doesn't collect anything about you.

What Cairn keeps, and where

Your name (for the morning greeting), your chosen categories and tone, your reminder times, your favorites, and the quotes you write or save all stay on your iPhone. They're shared only with Cairn's own widgets and share extension on the same device. Nothing is sent to me or to anyone else.

No internet, no accounts

Cairn never connects to the internet. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking, and no third-party code.

Reminders

Reminders are scheduled on your iPhone. There's no server sending them.

Tidy up

Tidy up uses Apple Intelligence on your iPhone to fix capitalization and punctuation. Your quote never leaves the device.

Your choices

You can change or delete anything in the app. Deleting Cairn removes everything it stored.

Children

Cairn collects no information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.

Contact

Questions? Write to support@wordstokeep.app.
