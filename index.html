Cairn

Quotes for the path ahead

A cairn is a small stack of stones that marks the trail. Cairn does the same for your day: one good line each hour, right where you'll see it.

  • In StandBy, and everywhere elseTurn your iPhone on its side while it charges to see the hour's quote. The same quote is on your Home Screen, your Lock Screen, and in the app.
  • Words that fit youChoose the themes you need and a tone: gentle, direct, or reflective. Each morning, a short greeting by name.
  • Save what speaks to youKeep favorites, write your own quotes, or save lines you find in other apps from the share sheet.
  • Yours, and only yoursNo accounts, no ads, and no tracking. Your quotes stay on your iPhone.
