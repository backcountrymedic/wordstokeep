Getting started

A cairn is a small stack of stones that marks the trail. Cairn does the same for your day: one good line each hour, right where you'll see it.

Your quote for the hour

Today shows one quote each hour. The same quote is on your widgets and in StandBy, so wherever you look, it matches.

  • Shuffle for a different quote this hour.
  • Share a link or a picture card.
  • Heart to keep a quote in Kept.
  • Hide (the eye) if you'd rather not see a quote again. Settings, Hidden quotes brings it back.

Put it where you'll see it

Home Screen: press and hold an empty spot, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and choose Cairn.

Lock Screen: press and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, then add a Cairn widget under the clock.

StandBy: turn your iPhone on its side while it charges. Swipe to the widgets, and Cairn shows the hour's quote on your nightstand.

Make it yours

In Settings you can choose:

  • Categories: the kinds of quotes you want, like Calm, Resilience, Trail, or Humor.
  • Tone: gentle, direct, or reflective.
  • Your name, for a short greeting each morning.
  • Reminders: how many a day, with a quiet chime, and quiet hours when Cairn stays silent.

Keep words that move you

Heard something worth remembering? Save it as your own.

  • Tap + on Today.
  • Say "Save a quote in Cairn" to Siri.
  • In Safari, Notes, or Books, select the text, tap Share, then Cairn.
  • Add New Quote to Control Center or the Action Button.

Your own quotes show up every few hours, alongside Cairn's.

Everything you keep

Kept holds your favorites, your own quotes, and quotes friends have shared with you. Tap any of them to show it on Today or share it.

Every so often, Cairn asks if you'd like to look back at a few old favorites.

Private by design

No accounts. No ads. No tracking. And no pop-ups asking you to rate the app. Ever. Your quotes stay on your iPhone.

One more thing: when you have a minute, open About Cairn at the bottom of Settings.
